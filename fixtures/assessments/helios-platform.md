# Helios Platform: assessment

<!-- policy: interpretive -->

## Where they threaten us

Highest threat in the set despite never appearing on a shortlist, because the
buyer already owns the licence. The competitive question is never features, it
is whether bundled controls are judged sufficient.

## Where we win

Mixed estates. Bundled controls stop at their own suite boundary and buyers with
data outside it discover the gap during evaluation.

## Where we lose

Single-suite buyers with no compliance driver. There is often no deal to win.

## Battlecard

**Objection:** "This is already included in our licence."
**Response:** Agree, then scope it. Ask which systems hold the data in question
and how many sit outside the suite. Do not argue the controls are bad; argue
about coverage boundaries.
